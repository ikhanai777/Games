How to play

Drag anywhere on the board to aim, release to fire your whole squad of balls. Keyboard: ← → aim, Space fire.


Each number is a brick's health. Every turn the bricks drop one row. If one reaches the floor the run ends, unless you revive.


◎ Green rings add a ball to your squad. ● Coins buy skins and upgrades.

⇿ ↕ Lasers hit a whole row or column every time a ball touches them. 🔥 Fire doubles a ball's damage. ✳ Splitters spawn ghost balls.


Bomb bricks blow up their neighbours (and chain!). Gold bricks drop coins. Crystal bricks give an extra ball. Every 15 levels a huge TITAN appears.


Kills in one turn build a combo multiplier up to ×5. Each hit plays the next note of a melody. Clear the whole board for a big bonus.


At level 5 and every 10 levels, choose one of three powers. Combine them into a build.
